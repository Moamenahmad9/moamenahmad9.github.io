HomeLedger — Privacy Policy

Effective 8 August 2026

HomeLedger is built on a simple principle: your financial data belongs to you, and only to you. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. HomeLedger's developer does not collect, receive, store, or have any access to your data. There are no servers behind the app.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — budgets, expenses, income, savings, projects, contractors, notes, and photos — is stored locally on your device.

iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is available and enabled, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account and synced by Apple between your own devices. It is protected by your Apple account and governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

Receipt scanning

When you photograph or upload a receipt, the text is read entirely on your device using Apple's built-in recognition technology. Receipt images never leave your phone.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Exports and sharing

PDF and CSV exports are created on your device and shared only when and where you choose to share them.

Third parties

HomeLedger contains no third-party software development kits, no advertising networks, and no analytics services.

Children

HomeLedger is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Since the app collects nothing, meaningful changes are unlikely.
